Select each pollution monster to inspect its full threat profile infographic.

THREAT PROFILE

Smogger

Smog-filled air makes it harder for communities and ecosystems to thrive. Smogger encapsulates toxic airborne pollutants and greenhouse emissions.

Plasticus

Discarded plastic travels through waterways and oceans, severely harming marine wildlife and lingering in ecosystems for centuries.

Oilblub

Industrial oil spills and toxic sludge suffocate marine life and degrade coastal water quality beyond rapid natural repair.

Toxitreaper

Toxic chemical runoff and industrial contamination destroy natural habitats, posing severe threats to living species.

Pollution Monster

The ultimate culmination of unchecked environmental damage, representing the final boss threat Firú must defeat to restore Earth.

The adventurer

Meet Firú

A brave young boy who transforms into a formidable warrior clad in silver steel armor, a red-crested knight helmet, and a flowing crimson cape. He holds a sword and shield, and carries a glowing, emerald-green Heart Crystal that radiates nature's magic.

The "Chosen One" and "Last Hope of Earth," appointed by the Spirit of Earth to fight against environmental destruction and purify corruptive forces.

Compassionate, resolute, and deeply attached to nature. He transitions from a simple village boy grieving over a dying world into an inspiring, action-oriented leader who unites humanity to heal and restore the planet.
